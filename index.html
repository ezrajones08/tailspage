Tails was ostracized on his home island due to his mutation for an extra tail. Sonic would save Tails from the bullying and help him embrace his uniqueness, which would be the inspiration Tails needed to begin flying using his tails. Tails constantly struggled with being stuck in Sonic’s shadow and relying on him rather than believing in his own ability. After journeying to the Starfall Islands with Sonic and Amy, he would be trapped on Chaos Island between the tangible world and the cyberspace developed by the Ancients. Sonic would free Tails and help him with an apprentice Koco developing weapon technology on the island. This Koco mirrored Tails’s relationship with Sonic and made him realize that he needs to travel on his own for some time to develop his independence and reach his full potential.
We often doubt our own abilities, especially when we compare ourselves to our peers. However, falling in the shadow of doubt can cause us to squander our own potential. Even through our failure, we must believe in ourselves and keep on following the path to our full potential.
